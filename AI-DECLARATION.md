# Declaration of AI assistance

**CSC4044Z Artificial Life, 2026: Reproducing Evolutionary Suicide**

Our group of three used Claude Code as an assistant while completing this project. We made the main research, design and experiment decisions, developed the project features, and interpreted the results. Claude Code helped us troubleshoot issues with our implementations, run the experiments, and analyse the results. We reviewed its assistance and remained responsible for the final work.

## Group contributions

We chose the research question, hypotheses and criteria for identifying evolutionary suicide. We selected the species and divided feature work among the three members: the Horn cell (Vimbai), Stripe cell (Moreblessing) and Cache cell (Ashley). We designed the shared soil-water and fertility system, decided on species traits and engine changes, and planned the experiments.

## Results and confirmation

The reported results come from Life Engine simulations and can be regenerated using the steps in the report. Outcomes are not hard-coded; deaths follow the simulation’s rules, while world files define the starting organisms and settings.
